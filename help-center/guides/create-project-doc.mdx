---
title: Creating Your First Doc Page
description: A short guide to making a documentation page in EWU.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Project">
    Select your project from the EWU dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add New Page">
    Click **"Add Page"**.
    Enter a title, then click **"Create Page"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Content">
    Use the rich-text editor to add headings, text, or images.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **"Publish"** to make your page visible to others.
  </Step>
</Steps>
