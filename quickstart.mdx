## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" title="Before You Begin">
Ensure you have:
- A valid email address
- Access to your email for verification
- A modern browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
</Callout>

Review these requirements to avoid issues during setup.

## Sign Up for an EWU Account

Follow these steps to create your account quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the EWU Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Navigate to `https://app.ewu.com` and click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details" icon="user">
    Provide your email, full name, and a strong password. Accept the terms of service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from EWU and click the link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Onboarding" icon="check-circle">
    Log in and complete the short onboarding survey to personalize your experience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your account is now ready. You'll land on the dashboard.

## Create Your First Documentation Space

Documentation spaces organize your project docs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="New Space" icon="plus">
    From the dashboard, click **New Space** and enter a name like `My Project Docs`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Template" icon="file-text">
    Select **Blank Space** for full control or **Starter Kit** for pre-built pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize Settings" icon="settings">
    Set visibility to `Private` or `Public`, add a description, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Spaces support unlimited pages, version history, and collaboration.
</Callout>

## Add Your First Page

Pages form the core of your documentation.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Markdown Page" icon="file">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create Page">
        In your space, click **New Page** > **Markdown**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add Content">
        Use the editor to write. Here's a starter:
      </Step>
    </Steps>

````mdx
# Welcome to My Docs

This is your first page.

## Quick Links

- [Getting Started](/quickstart)
- [Authentication](/authentication)
````
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rich Text Page" icon="edit-3">
    Use the WYSIWYG editor for non-technical content. Add headings, lists, and embeds directly.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Publish by clicking **Save & Publish**.

## Initial Customization Tips

<Expandable title="Brand Your Space" default-open="true">
Set a custom domain, logo, and colors via **Space Settings** > **Branding**. Use your brand color `#F89E17` for accents.

Upload a favicon and sidebar image for a professional look.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Invite Collaborators">
Share edit access by entering emails in **Members** tab. Set roles like Editor or Viewer.
</Expandable>
